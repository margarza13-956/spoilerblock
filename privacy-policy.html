🛡️ SpoilerBlock Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

Summary: SpoilerBlock is built with a privacy-first, zero-data-collection architecture. All keyword matching, feed scanning, and settings storage occur 100% locally in your browser. We do not track you, collect personal data, or transmit your browsing information to any external server.

1. Information Collection and Use

SpoilerBlock does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information, browsing history, user feeds, or watchlist keywords to any remote servers. Specifically:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register an account or provide any personal details to use SpoilerBlock.
  • No Analytics or Telemetry: We do not track your browsing habits, page visits, clicks, or extension interactions.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: No data is ever shared with, sold to, or monetized by third parties or advertisers.

2. Local Storage (`chrome.storage.local`)

SpoilerBlock utilizes standard browser local storage (`chrome.storage.local`) solely on your device to persist:

  • Your custom watchlist titles and keyword filters.
  • Your local configuration preferences (e.g., match mode, blur strength, pause toggle).
  • Aggregated local statistics (e.g., total count of posts blocked on your device).

This data never leaves your computer and is immediately erased if you uninstall the extension or clear your browser data.

3. Permissions Justification

  • `storage`: Required strictly to save your watchlist items and user preferences locally in your browser.
  • `activeTab`: Required to allow the popup to interact with the active social media tab when triggered.
  • Host Permissions (`facebook.com`, `x.com`, `twitter.com`, `reddit.com`): Required solely to inject the local content script (`content.js`) and stylesheet (`spoilerblock.css`) on supported social platforms to detect and blur spoiler posts in real-time.

4. Third-Party Websites

SpoilerBlock operates on supported social media platforms (such as Facebook, X, and Reddit). We do not control and are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third-party websites.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the official GitHub repository: github.com/margarza13-956/spoilerblock.
